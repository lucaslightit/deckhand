The big decision

Three strategies, none chosen

StrategyHow it worksRiskCoexistence cost
Big-bangOne cutover weekend, every tenant at onceHigh, concentratedLow
Rolling per tenantOne tenant at a timeMedium, spreadHigh: both systems live for months
Shadow / CDCNew platform receives legacy writes; reads flip first, then writesLowest data-loss riskHardest to build
The right one depends on two things we cannot measure yet: the real size of legacy and team bandwidth.
